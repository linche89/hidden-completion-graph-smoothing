Keywords:                                                This paper introduces several related distributed algorithms, generalised from the celebrated belief propagation
Distributed estimation                                   algorithm for statistical learning. These algorithms are suitable for a class of computational problems in large-
Distributed optimisation                                 scale networked systems, ranging from average consensus, sensor fusion, distributed estimation, distributed
Sensor fusion

Distributed algorithm

                                                         the desired solution. The behaviours of these algorithms are discussed in terms of the network graph topology
                                                         and parameters of the corresponding computational problem. A number of examples are presented to illustrate
                                                         their applications. Also introduced is a message-passing algorithm for distributed convex optimisation.
